## 👨‍💻 About me

I'm a **Backend Developer** at **Next Shipping Logística Internacional**, where I build, maintain and evolve corporate systems — **ERP**, **CRM** and an **LMS (learning platform)** — plus integrations with third-party systems for the international logistics segment.

I have solid experience with **REST APIs**, business rule implementation, database modeling and **SQL Server (T-SQL)** query optimization. Day to day I work with **Node.js**, **TypeScript**, **AdonisJS**, **SQL Server**, **MySQL** and **MongoDB**, with working knowledge of **Python** and **PHP**.

- 🏢 Currently at **Next Shipping** — evolving the ERP, CRM, Next Academy (LMS) and the HeadCargo ERP
- 🎓 Bachelor's in **Software Engineering** (Faculdade da Indústria Afonso Pena, 2022–2025)
- 📚 Currently pursuing a **Postgraduate degree in Data Science**
- 🔭 Expanding my knowledge of **C#/.NET** and **Java**
- 📝 Author of the paper *"The Influence of Data Democracy on Organizational Agility"* — Revista Inova Mais, 2024
- 📍 São José dos Pinhais, PR — Brazil
